# 12 — Future Improvements & Architecture Roadmap

## 1. Short-Term Enhancements (Next 1-3 Months)
- <strong style="color: #E26D5C; font-weight: 600;">Live Serverless Contact Function:</strong> <span style="color: #B2CEBD;">Connect the contact form to a live serverless backend (Netlify Functions or Cloudflare Workers) linked to an email relay (Resend or SendGrid) with turnkey captcha verification.</span>
- <strong style="color: #E26D5C; font-weight: 600;">Interactive Terminal Sandbox:</strong> <span style="color: #B2CEBD;">Add an interactive terminal drawer where visitors can type commands (`cat about.txt`, `run audit`, `ping projects`) for a gamified technical experience.</span>
